Introduction

Minicube-II is a minimal configuration. It is the modular robot with the minimum number of modules capable of moving in 2D.

To build it, the following parts are needed:

  • One MY1 module (Assembly instructions)
  • One Unimod 2D type B1 (Assembly instructions)
  • 4 M3 bolts. Length: 8mm
  • 4 M3 nuts
