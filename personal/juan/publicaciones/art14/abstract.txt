Locomotion Capabilities of a Modular Robot with Eight Pitch-Yaw-Connecting Modules

Abstract:

In this paper, a general classification of the modular robots is proposed, based on their
topology and the type of connection between the modules. The locomotion capabilities of the
sub-group of pitch-yaw connecting robots are analyzed. Five different gaits have been
implemented and tested on a real robot composed of eight modules. One of them, rotating,
has not been previously achieved. All gaits are implemented using a simple and elegant
central pattern generator (CPG) approach that simplify the algorithms of the controlling
system.
